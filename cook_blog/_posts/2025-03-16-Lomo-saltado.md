## Ingredients

- ¾ kilo of beef
- 1 garlic piece, minced
- 1 red onion (big) cut in large pieces
- 2 table spoons of “Aji Amarillo” sauce
- 2 tomatoes cut in big, long pieces
- ½ kilo of French fries
- 2 table spoons of red vinegar
- 2 table spoons of soy sauce

## Preparation

Cut the beef in cubes, season it with salt, pepper and the garlic. Then, in a pan, put oil and start frying the meat. Leave it frying for a while and then add the onions. After that, add the red vinegar and the Aji Amarillo sauce. Keep cooking, add the tomatoes and after that add the soy sauce.

Also, put in the oven the French fries. Set the oven to 220° C for around 20 minutes.

Now it is ready to be served with the French fires and white rice!
